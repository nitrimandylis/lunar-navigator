# Story: research and presentation preparation

This folder contains the team's research notes, source register and working drafts for explaining Lunar Navigator and preparing its NASA Space Apps submission.

## How we use references

NASA's current challenge, submission requirements and judging rubric are the primary references. Credited past projects and professional accounts are additional examples for studying how to communicate a problem, explain evidence and describe a user experience.

Our notes distinguish the original creators' work and claims from our observations and proposed applications. Reference projects can inspire presentation principles. Project-specific concepts, wording, visuals, methods and results are attributed to the original creators, with our own contributions and any adaptations described separately. Reused material must be specifically credited and used with the appropriate permissions.

The final submission should present our team's actual work, verified outputs and limitations. Research notes, illustrative examples and unverified plans in this folder are not claims of implemented functionality or final project results.
